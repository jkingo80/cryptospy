A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Crypto SPY questions

Does Crypto SPY use real money?

No. Trading and predictions use virtual money. Crypto SPY does not buy, sell or hold real cryptocurrency.

Is Crypto SPY free?

The app is free to download, with optional Premium in-app purchases. Check the App Store and the app for current pricing, availability and subscription terms.

Which devices can I use?

Crypto SPY is available for iPhone and iPad. The App Store lists current device and operating system compatibility.

Can I use Crypto SPY to learn about crypto?

Crypto SPY lets you explore market prices, practice virtual trades and try prediction challenges. It is an educational game; its content is not financial advice.
